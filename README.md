# 🛒 Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to uncover insights into purchasing patterns, spending habits, customer demographics, product preferences, and satisfaction levels. The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using **Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to answer key business questions such as:

- Which customer segments contribute the highest revenue?
- Which product categories are most popular?
- How do age and gender influence purchasing behavior?
- Which payment methods are preferred by customers?
- What factors impact customer satisfaction?
- How can businesses improve customer retention and sales?

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Young and middle-aged customers contribute a significant share of purchases.
- Certain product categories consistently generate higher revenue.
- Digital payment methods are the most frequently used.
- Customer satisfaction varies across product categories.
- Repeat customers contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Customer demographics influence purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
